# VISION — Unified Content Studio

## Current Status: Merged Platform v6.1
ContentCommand AI + Legacy One Universal Content Platform merged into a single unified content studio.

## Ultimate Goal
Create the ultimate all-in-one AI-powered hub for social media creators, marketing agencies, and scaling businesses — supporting **any business type** (Real Estate, E-Commerce, Restaurant, General) with dynamic, context-aware content generation.

## Foundational Concepts

### From ContentCommand AI
1. **Multi-Agent Workspace:** Natural language commands → draft posts, video scripts, podcast outlines, brand kits, landing pages.
2. **Robust Integration:** Twitter, LinkedIn, Meta, YouTube, TikTok via PKCE OAuth.
3. **RAG:** Ground AI generations with live web facts and image context.
4. **Data-Centric Tracking:** Real engagement stats via backend analytics.

### From Legacy One Universal Content Platform
5. **Universality:** Not hardcoded to a single industry. Dynamic schemas, APIs, and UI based on selected business type.
6. **Autonomous Automation:** AI schedules bulk content over weeks/months for consistent presence.
7. **Immediate Value:** Generate and schedule a week's worth of content within 30 seconds.
8. **AI Persona:** Brand voice from past successful posts injected into every generation.

## User-Satisfaction Design
- Vibrant modern styling, dark interfaces, glassmorphism
- Context-aware tooltips and help menus
- Centralized Learning Center
- Drag-to-select calendar for fast scheduling
- Draft review before anything goes live
- Canva integration for design polish
