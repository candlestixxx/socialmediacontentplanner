# Compliance Guidelines for ContentCommand AI

## 1. Web Research and Scraping
- DO NOT perform illegal web scraping.
- Respect `robots.txt`.
- Only use official News APIs, Search APIs, or RSS feeds.
- Rely on user-provided public URLs where allowed by terms.

## 2. Platform Policies (Meta, TikTok, X, etc.)
- Do not automate spam posting.
- Honor specific platform character limits and media restrictions.
- Ensure that users re-authenticate via OAuth flow when tokens expire.
- Comply with all developer terms of service for connected platforms.

## 3. AI Generated Content & Copyright
- Always inject a transformation prompt for AI output to avoid direct plagiarism.
- Alert the user if the AI response closely mirrors the source input.
- Require users to explicitly authorize "Auto-Publish" features to avoid accidental unauthorized disclosures.

## 4. Advertising & Sponsored Content
- Provide checkboxes for "Sponsored Content" disclosures.
- Include tax/legal disclaimers when showing the Finance/Analytics revenue estimator. The platform does not provide legal or licensed tax accounting advice.
