# ContentCommand AI - User Manual

Welcome to ContentCommand AI, your all-in-one hub for creating, scheduling, and tracking your social media presence.

## 1. Getting Started

### Connecting Social Accounts
1. Navigate to the **Social Accounts** tab from the main dashboard.
2. Click **Connect Account** and authorize ContentCommand via the standard login flow (Facebook, X, LinkedIn).
3. The system securely encrypts your connection tokens. You can disconnect at any time.

### Setting up a Brand Kit
1. Go to **Brand Kit**.
2. Define your voice rules (e.g., "Professional but friendly"), preferred hex colors, and banned words.
3. The AI Generation Studios will automatically apply these settings when creating content.

## 2. Generating Content

### AI Post Studio
1. Open the **AI Studio**.
2. Enter a topic (e.g., "AI Marketing Trends for 2026").
3. Select your desired tone and platforms.
4. Click **Generate**. The system will output platform-specific content (e.g., short punchy text for X, longer formatted text for LinkedIn).

### Video & Podcast Studios
- **Video Studio:** Enter a topic to generate a 30-second scene-by-scene Reel/TikTok script.
- **Podcast Studio:** Generate full show outlines, guest questions, and automated YouTube descriptions.

## 3. Scheduling and Campaigns

### The Campaign Planner
1. Navigate to **Campaigns**.
2. Create a new campaign spanning specific dates (e.g., "Q4 Holiday Push").
3. As you generate content in the AI Studio, attach it to your Campaign.
4. Use the **Calendar** to drag and drop content into specific time slots. The system will handle the automated posting in the background.

## 4. Tracking Success

### Analytics Engine
- View the **Dashboard** for a snapshot of your scheduled posts and high-level metrics.
- ContentCommand aggregates likes, comments, and shares across platforms.

### Finance & Reports
1. Go to **Billing & Finance**.
2. Add your external ad-spend (e.g., Meta Ads).
3. The dashboard will estimate your Net Profit.
4. *Disclaimer: All financial data is estimated. Export CSVs for your certified accountant.*
